Explore the beautiful landscapes, culture and attractions of Lesotho.

Katse Dam

A major dam surrounded by the mountains of Lesotho.

Basotho Blanket

A traditional blanket that represents Basotho culture.

Maliba Lodge

Mountain accommodation in the Tsehlanyane area.

Maletsunyane Falls

A famous waterfall near Semonkong.

Liphofung Cave

A cultural and historical site with San rock art.

Sehlabathebe National Park

A protected area known for its natural scenery.

Thaba-Bosiu

A historic mountain linked to King Moshoeshoe I.

Sani Pass

A spectacular mountain pass in the eastern highlands.

Pony Trekking

Pony trekking allows visitors to explore Lesotho's beautiful mountains on horseback.

Ts'ehlanyane National Park

Ts'ehlanyane National Park is known for its mountain scenery, natural vegetation and beautiful landscapes.

Mohale Dam

Visit Mohale Dam to enjoy beautiful mountain views, peaceful surroundings and scenic landscapes. It is a wonderful place to appreciate Lesotho's natural beauty.

Afriski Mountain Resort

A highland resort known for winter sports and mountain activities.

Avani Maseru

A hotel in Maseru offering accommodation for visitors.

Kome Caves

Kome Caves are famous for their traditional cave dwellings and cultural history.

Dinosaur Footprints

Lesotho has ancient dinosaur footprints preserved in rock, showing signs of life from millions of years ago.
