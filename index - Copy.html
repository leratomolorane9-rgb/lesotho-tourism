Welcome to Lesotho - The Kingdom in the Sky!

Step into the Kingdom of Lesotho, the only country in the world situated above 1,000 metres above sea level, with spectacular scenery, towering mountains, captivating culture and unforgettable memories. Enjoy the mountain scenery of Sani Pass, marvel at magnificent Maletsunyane Falls, and explore the cultural heritage of Thaba-Bosiu.

Experience the beauty of Lesotho

Lesotho welcomes different types of tourists. Culture tourists can learn about Basotho history and traditions, nature lovers can admire the beautiful landscapes, and winter sports tourists can enjoy skiing and snowboarding.

Popular Tourist Attractions

  • Thaba-Bosiu Cultural Village
  • Maletsunyane Falls
  • Katse Dam
  • Sehlabathebe National Park
  • Afriski Mountain Resort

Each destination offers visitors a different way to experience the natural beauty, history and culture of Lesotho.
