About Lesotho

Learn about our history, people and mountain kingdom.

Where is Lesotho?

Lesotho is a landlocked country completely surrounded by South Africa. Its capital city is Maseru. The country is known for its high mountains, valleys, rivers and waterfalls. Its mountainous landscape is an important part of its identity and attracts visitors who enjoy nature and outdoor activities.

How Did Lesotho Become an Independent Country?

The history of Lesotho is closely linked to King Moshoeshoe I, who brought different communities together in the early 1800s to form the Basotho nation. Thaba-Bosiu became an important place for his people to live and find protection during conflicts. Lesotho came under British protection in 1868. On 4 October 1966, it gained independence and became the country it is today. Its history remains an important part of Basotho identity and heritage.

Who Are the Basotho?

The people of Lesotho are called Basotho, and one person is called a Mosotho. Sesotho and English are the official languages. Family, community, respect and kindness towards people are important parts of Basotho life. Visitors can learn about local traditions through cultural sites, crafts, music and everyday interactions with local communities.

What Makes Basotho Culture Special?

Basotho culture can be seen in traditional clothing, music, dancing and crafts. The Basotho blanket is worn for warmth and is also an important cultural symbol. The Mokorotlo, a traditional straw hat, is another recognised symbol of Lesotho.

  • Traditional music and dancing
  • Basotho blankets and traditional clothing
  • Handmade crafts
  • The Mokorotlo hat

Why Visit Lesotho?

Lesotho offers visitors a chance to experience mountain scenery, learn about Basotho history and culture, and explore places that are different from busy cities. Tourists can visit historical sites, admire waterfalls, explore national parks and enjoy activities in the mountains. These experiences make Lesotho a destination for visitors interested in nature, culture and adventure.
