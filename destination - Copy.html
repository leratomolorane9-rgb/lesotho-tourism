Discover the Best of Lesotho

Lesotho is a beautiful mountain Kingdom offering historical attractions, spectacular waterfalls, exciting outdoor activities and unique cultural experiences.

Thaba-Bosiu

Location: Maseru District

Historical sandstone plateau where King Moshoeshoe I established his stronghold in the 19th century.

  • Explore historical mountain
  • Visit sites of Basotho nation
  • Guided tours
Maletsunyane Falls

Location: Near Semonkong, Maseru District

One of Lesotho's most spectacular waterfalls - 192 metres plunge surrounded by rugged mountains.

  • Admire waterfall viewpoints
  • Photography
  • Hiking routes
Katse Dam

Location: Malibamat'so River, Thaba-Tseka

Major part of Lesotho Highlands Water Project, recognised for its concrete arch wall.

  • Admire dam wall
  • Scenic drives
  • Learn about LHWP
Afriski Mountain Resort

Location: Mahlasela Pass, Butha-Buthe

Known for winter sports, skiing, snowboarding, high-altitude scenery.

  • Skiing and snowboarding
  • Winter lessons
  • Snow-capped mountains
Sehlabathebe National Park

Location: Qacha's Nek District

Established 1969, oldest park, part of Maloti-Drakensberg World Heritage Site.

  • Hiking
  • Bird-watching
  • Rock formations
